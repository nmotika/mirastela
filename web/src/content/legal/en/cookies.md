## What we use

Mirastela currently uses only what is strictly necessary for the website to work. These do not need your consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mirastela-funnel | Session storage in your browser | Remembers your answers while you go through the steps, so that you do not lose them on reload | Until you close the tab |
| __cf_bm, cf_clearance | Cookies set by Cloudflare | Protect the website against bots and attacks | Up to 30 minutes / up to 1 year |

## Analytics and advertising

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies at the moment. If we introduce them, for example to measure visits or the results of our ads, we will first ask for your consent with a clear banner, and we will list them on this page. You will be able to change your choice at any time.

## How to remove them

You can delete cookies and stored data at any time in your browser settings. The site will still work, but your answers in an unfinished questionnaire will be lost.
