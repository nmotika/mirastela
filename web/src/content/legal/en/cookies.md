## What we use

These are strictly necessary for the website to work and do not need your consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| mirastela-funnel | Session storage in your browser | Remembers your answers while you go through the steps, so that you do not lose them on reload | Until you close the tab |
| __cf_bm, cf_clearance | Cookies set by Cloudflare | Protect the website against bots and attacks | Up to 30 minutes / up to 1 year |
| mirastela-consent | Local storage in your browser | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you delete it |
| mirastela-account | Local storage in your browser | Keeps you signed in on "My reports" after you open the sign-in link | Until you sign out, at most 90 days |
| mirastela-partner | Local storage in your browser | Remembers the partner code if you arrive through a partner link, so your discount applies and the partner gets credit for the referral | 30 days |

## Analytics and advertising

Only if you click "Accept all" in the cookie banner do we load the Meta pixel, which measures how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform (for example, whether someone who saw an ad later bought a report). With "Necessary only" we load neither the Meta pixel nor Google Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies, is described in the Privacy policy. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

We also load Google Analytics, which shows how visitors use the site (which pages they open, where they leave the questionnaire, how many buy). Google receives your IP address, which it shortens, and browser details, but not your email or birth data.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _fbp, _fbc | Cookies set by Meta | Recognise the browser and the ad click to measure ad results | Up to 90 days |
| _ga, _ga_* | Cookies set by Google Analytics | Distinguish visitors and visits for site statistics | Up to 2 years |

## How to remove them

You can delete cookies and stored data at any time in your browser settings. The site will still work, but your answers in an unfinished questionnaire will be lost.
