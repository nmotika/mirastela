## 2. What Mirastela offers

Mirastela calculates astrological charts from the birth data you enter (date, time and place of birth) using astronomical ephemerides, and writes personal texts about them. We offer:

- **Free content:** daily horoscopes for each sign and a short summary of your natal chart.
- **One-time digital products:** the full natal chart report, the compatibility (synastry) report with a partner, the yearly forecast, the natal chart of a child, and bundles of these products.
- **Subscription:** the personal astrological calendar (a daily rating and short tip in your phone calendar) together with a weekly personal horoscope by email.

The texts are written with the help of artificial intelligence, based on the calculated positions of the planets.
