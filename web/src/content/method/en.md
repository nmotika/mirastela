# How we work

Mirastela is an astrology service run by Something Digital d.o.o. from Zagreb, Croatia. This page explains where the numbers on the site come from and how the texts are written.

## Calculations

Planet positions, the Ascendant and the houses are calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris, the astronomical library also used by professional astrology software. We use the tropical zodiac and Placidus houses (above the polar circles, where Placidus does not work, whole-sign houses). For a birth chart we use the date, time and place of birth with the local time zone.

## How the texts are written

Horoscopes, sign descriptions, yearly forecasts and personal reports are written by an AI language model (Claude, by Anthropic), only from data we calculate first: which planet is in which sign, when it changes sign and which area of life (solar house) it touches for each sign. The model does not calculate anything itself, so the dates and positions in the texts match the sky. The daily horoscope is written every morning from that day's sky.

## What astrology is, and is not

Astrology is a symbolic language for reflection, not a science and not a prediction of events. Our texts are not advice on health, money, law or relationships. For those, talk to a qualified professional.
